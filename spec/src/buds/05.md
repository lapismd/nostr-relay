## Lapis implementation

`HEAD /media` checks the required hash, size, MIME, signed `media` authorization, and relay write policy. `PUT /media` verifies the request-body hash, decodes bounded static JPEG or PNG input, re-encodes it, and retains the shorter of the processed and original representations. The response includes a BUD-08 NIP-94 tag set. `GET /media` retains its legacy redirect to `/upload`.

| Source requirement | Lapis behavior | Classification | Reason and compatibility impact | Evidence/status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Static image optimization | `PUT` accepts JPEG and PNG; other formats return `415`. Invalid or oversized decoded images return `422`. | Partial | This is an intentional static-image subset. Animated images and video have no safe processing pipeline. | `internal/blossom/media.go`, `internal/blossom/server_test.go` |
| Preflight | `HEAD` accepts JPEG and PNG metadata, but cannot guarantee the bytes will decode or that the later write succeeds. | Implemented | This is the optional advisory check defined by BUD-05. | `internal/blossom/media.go` |
