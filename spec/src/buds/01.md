## Lapis implementation

The relay exposes GET and HEAD for hash paths, supports optional extensions and HTTP range reads, and applies one authorization and visibility policy to both methods. Successful reads expose the stored MIME type and length plus identical ETag, cache, range and CORS headers; HEAD omits the body.
